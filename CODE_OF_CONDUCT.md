# Community conduct

nightfall!punk collaboration should be rigorous, welcoming, and materially useful.

Participants are expected to critique ideas, code, evidence, and decisions without harassment or personal degradation; attribute others' work; disclose relevant conflicts of interest; respect privacy and consent; avoid discriminatory treatment; and correct mistakes transparently.

Disagreement is expected. Evidence should be challenged through reproducible counterexamples, review, and reasoned argument rather than status, intimidation, or pile-ons.

Project maintainers may moderate contributions or participation that undermine safety, collaboration, legal compliance, or research integrity. Decisions should be documented proportionately and may be appealed through ordinary project governance.

This is original project policy text, not a copy of an external code-of-conduct template.
