# nightfall!punk — Ethics, Consequence & Spectacle Contract

Status: **accepted design contract / pre-implementation**

Date: 2026-08-10

This contract records the ethical and presentation boundary for nightfall!punk. It does not authorize a compile, merge, morality system, censorship subsystem, or new gameplay feature by itself.

## Governing distinction

nightfall!punk is an **ethical game** in the sense that its world, stories, systems and consequences can contain ethical lessons, ethical possibilities, solidarity, care, stewardship, reciprocity, exploitation, betrayal, cruelty, repair and responsibility.

It is **not propaganda for ethics**. The game should not force the player into a single approved moral script, reward all ethical behavior with automatic bonuses, or convert moral judgment into an omniscient score.

Ethics is a normative suggestion and a field of consequences, not an authoritarian command layer.

Canonical maxim:

> **The game may teach through consequence without preaching through compulsory moral scripting.**

## Player and actor freedom

Within the game's content constraints and taboos, actors may behave ethically, ambiguously, selfishly, opportunistically, exploitatively, cruelly, irresponsibly or otherwise unethically.

Unethical action is therefore not a simulation error. It is a legitimate possibility inside the Lifeworld.

Do not implement:

- universal `GOOD/EVIL` alignment;
- morality points;
- automatic karma;
- omniscient ethical labels on ordinary actions;
- mandatory ethical dialogue outcomes;
- hidden Director punishment simply because an action is judged immoral.

Prefer:

`action -> material/social consequence -> trace/memory -> changed relation -> changed future possibility`

The world answers conduct through the same causal substrate used elsewhere: injury, access, trust, refusal, retaliation, cooperation, debt, maintenance, damaged infrastructure, changed routes, reputation evidence, bargaining positions, institutional drift, ecological consequences and persistent traces.

## Ethical consequence is not moral propaganda

A harmful action may have harmful consequences because of what it physically and socially does, not because a morality engine decides to punish the actor.

Likewise, an ethical action is not guaranteed to succeed or be rewarded. Care, solidarity, restraint or stewardship may be costly, misunderstood, exploited, unsuccessful or locally irrational while still having genuine ethical significance.

Therefore keep distinct:

`ethical value != tactical success != economic payoff != social approval != survival`

and:

`unethical behavior != guaranteed failure`

The simulation should preserve the possibility of tragic, ambiguous, contradictory and mixed outcomes.

## Structural ethics

The existing structural-violence and social-ecology contracts remain binding:

- a concrete situated action may itself instantiate a structural relation without exhausting the macro totality;
- structural violence is not stored as a master scalar;
- intent does not monopolize the meaning of an action;
- responsibility, knowledge, agency, dependency and alternatives remain distinct determinations;
- micro and macro relations are constitutively connected but analytically nonidentical;
- useful institutions can also reproduce domination;
- ethical resistance can coexist with partial reproduction of the structure being resisted.

Ethical interpretation therefore arises from situated relations and histories rather than faction essence or morality tags.

## PG-13 / gore-free constraint

The game may depict intense, frightening, unethical and violent situations while maintaining a **PG-13, gore-free presentation boundary**.

The intended spectacle is not realistic gore. It is **goo cyberpunk horror** and adjacent non-gory horror grammars:

- paranormal horror;
- psychological horror;
- thriller tension;
- surreal cyberpunk contamination;
- synthetic residue;
- petroleum-violet / blue-black infestation;
- smoke, filaments, static, anomalous light and impossible material behavior;
- gore-free body horror expressed through deformation, silhouette, movement, contamination, transformation, loss of agency, uncanny anatomy or synthetic material effects rather than explicit viscera.

The aesthetic should communicate injury, corruption, predation, vulnerability and horror without relying on explicit blood-and-guts spectacle.

Presentation maxim:

> **Horror is carried by consequence, transformation, atmosphere and impossible materiality—not by gore.**

## Ethical and unethical spectacle

Ethical and unethical behavior should both remain physically and socially legible.

Examples of consequence channels include:

- a spared or rescued actor later remembers the intervention;
- exploitation changes access, disagreement points and institutional expectations;
- betrayal changes future cooperation and evidentiary trust;
- reckless violence damages infrastructure, routes, resources or social relations;
- stewardship preserves or restores commons and future access;
- intimidation may succeed tactically while degrading future cooperation;
- mercy may create future reciprocity, ambiguity, vulnerability or no immediate reward at all;
- mercenary work may materially reproduce relations a Crew culturally opposes;
- Dream Cattler violence remains ecological/predatory rather than human moral grammar.

The goal is not to tell the player what to think through an overlay. The goal is to make actions **matter enough that interpretation becomes possible**.

## Ghost Static / Apophis boundary

Ghost Static may frame, publicize, interpret or culturally amplify the consequences of events through legitimate diegetic media and bounded Director authority. Ghost Static does not assign hidden morality scores or rewrite cognition.

Apophis may establish explicit authored narrative invariants when required by story mode, but ordinary ethical consequence should remain Lifeworld-causal rather than deus-ex-machina punishment/reward.

## Implementation consequence

When an ethical-system implementation is eventually earned, extend existing relation/evidence systems rather than adding a morality engine.

Preferred chain:

`actor intention + situated action -> ordinary world consequence -> trace/memory -> actor interpretation -> social/material response -> future gradient`

Useful existing substrates include:

- `nf_lifeworld` causal memory;
- energy accounting and sustainable viability;
- access and topography;
- actor belief / epistemic confidence;
- situated disagreement points;
- trust/reliability evidence;
- stigmergic traces;
- property/commons/stewardship relations;
- structural-constraint evidence;
- damage, stasis, repair and persistent environmental consequence.

## Complexity guard

Do not add merely to represent ethics:

- morality meters;
- faction-wide virtue scores;
- universal guilt/shame variables;
- Director-authored karma;
- guaranteed reward for prosocial behavior;
- guaranteed punishment for antisocial behavior;
- explicit gore escalation as substitute for consequence;
- ethical tutorial popups explaining the intended lesson.

## Final contract

> **nightfall!punk is ethically meaningful without being ethical propaganda. It permits bounded unethical behavior, preserves actor agency, and lets the Lifeworld answer conduct through physical, ecological, economic and social consequence. Its violence and horror remain PG-13 and gore-free, expressed through goo-cyberpunk, paranormal, psychological, thriller and synthetic body-horror spectacle rather than explicit gore.**
