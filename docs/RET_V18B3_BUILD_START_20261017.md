## Research programme RP-B3
Question: Can seven physical-interaction families realize events as actual material consequences under authoritative A6 tick control, with correct contracts, A3/A4/A5 causality and bounded responsiveness, without new parallel ownership or overbuilt cross-build infrastructure?

Hard core: eligibility is necessary but never sufficient for execution; EEC-01 event and historical consequence preserved; actor/target/action identity and epoch-binding; physically applied contacts only; no fabricated state, history or energy; fail-closed pending; no double transfer/debit; explicit interruption and compensation. Scope: B3 door, switch, pickup, push, carry, repair and interruption. Defer long-run cross-actor strategic fairness to B4 and networked crash-atomic registry to B5.

Operational semantics:
- Door: realized hinge/slider displacement plus collision/latch state; jam, obstruction and forced reversal supported as bounded failures.
- Switch: physically valid activation produces exactly one causally attributed signal/state event; unknown power/preconditions pending.
- Pickup: support/possession transition and inventory/attachment change must be one authoritative transaction.
- Push: real A4 reciprocal impulse against a dynamic object with A5 history, no fake kinematic body response.
- Carry: dynamic/constraint-based attachment with legitimate collision, support and impulse exchanges; release/interruption restores actual physical consequences.
- Repair: real finite work, material/resource debit and condition improvement; partial progress and interruption preserve causal history.
- Interruption: precommit cancel has no physical effect, postcommit history remains and compensation is new event, not erasure.
