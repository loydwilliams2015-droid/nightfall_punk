## Q569 — KEEP: Relational Action Block encounter topology

Use a compact, readable encounter block combining industry-proven FPS spatial clarity with nightfall relational-information geometry.

Required spatial relations:
- three meaningful route choices;
- a contested center;
- at least one occluding divider that produces different legitimate sight histories;
- one safer but slower detour;
- one elevated or otherwise advantaged information vantage;
- one route/resource interaction whose state materially changes passage value.

The encounter must be small enough that divergent actor knowledge is diagnosable, but rich enough that attack, guard, reposition, investigate, and withdrawal can all be rational.

## Q570 — KEEP: Peripheral but causally real Dream Cattler pressure

Dream Cattlers are present but not the center of the first v1.5 communications proof.

Use one or two Cattlers as readable ecological pressure that can alter route utility, risk, or future options without converting the encounter into a three-way showcase.

Invariant:

`HumanCommunication != CattlerSignaling`

Cattlers retain their own ecological perception/signaling ontology while participating in the same world consequences.

## Q571 — KEEP: Sustainable Passage objective

The player's immediate proof objective is not simple elimination.

Use a small contested resupply/energy node whose activation or stabilization materially improves or maintains a passage route. The player may secure, traverse, hold, disengage, or fight according to the changing relation.

Priority remains lexicographic:

1. objective / transition;
2. viability;
3. sustainable energy/resource position;
4. future options;
5. damage/cost efficiency.

Damage remains instrumental rather than the victory axis.

## Q572 — KEEP: Modestly differentiated Human Rival dispositions

Both Human Rivals use the same AI machinery, evidence model, report system, nested-account projection, and action set.

Use only modest role/disposition bias:
- Rival A: slightly favors route/guard continuity;
- Rival B: slightly favors probing/information pressure.

The difference must remain small enough that Information Topography is the dominant experimental variable.

Desired relation:

`DifferentAction = InformationDifference + ModestDispositionDifference`

not:

`DifferentAction = CompletelyDifferentScript`
