# C05LINE — Public Physical Eligibility and Selective Refinement Commitment
Date: 2026-10-18. PRAXIS / SIGNAL / STEWARD; 1.8B.7A Group 1. Public engineering-policy lock, not full physics integration or empirical gate closure.

## Governing commitment
**Prefer simple, high-fidelity, material-grounded eligibility under a common world coordinate system; activate finer spatial checks only when justified by clearance margins, geometry complexity, moving contacts, material change or unresolved evidence.** A3 geometry, A5 material evidence and A6 single world-tick authority remain distinct; B1/B2 permission never entails actual B3/A4/A5 realized consequences. Any insufficient or stale material proof remains PENDING or fails closed, never silently approved. Fidelity is measured by causal correctness and false-approval rate, not refinement frequency.

## Three procedures
A Physical eligibility: verify real actor/body/tool swept clearance, reach, support, material solidity/stability and contract permission independently; no point-probe shortcut may overrule full A3 geometry.
B Selective dynamic revalidation: ordinary authoritative sparse 1 m cell/nexus checks where adequate; allocate existing 0.25–0.5 m local refinement islands only around narrow gaps, collision ambiguity, damage, changing support or consequential contact. Use deterministic thresholds, budget-limited work, explicit PENDING on unresolved work; refine *before* a positive grant when coarse checks cannot establish safety. Revalidate geometry/material/world epochs immediately prior to publication under A6 authority.
C Falsification and optimization: compare incumbent coarse+selective refinement to high-fidelity reference and always-fine challenger on paired canonical/random/pathological/boundary/held-out samples; measure wrong positive/negative, causal precision/recall, actual queries, CPU p50/p95/p99, memory, backlog and responsiveness. Never invent speed advantage or treat UI visualization as physical witness.

## Hard invariants
1. Unknown, contradictory or obsolete evidence cannot authorize a physical action.
2. Refined cells share original world coordinates and canonical parent identity; changing scale cannot duplicate materials/resources/objects.
3. Collision, support and reach must hold for actor swept envelope and relevant movable geometry, not solely centerpoints.
4. Refinement cannot introduce a second A6 authoritative tick owner, nor let semantic connectivity expand physical solver islands.
5. A successful eligible witness is not a committed, executed or durable consequence (C04 lifecycle).
6. Explicit bounded fallback: too expensive or indeterminate -> PENDING, with bounded retry and reason trace; no invisible grant.
7. A physical implementation and trusted positive/negative fixtures are necessary before marking PASS.

## RET and acceptance
A priori: lacking evidence cannot rationally certify a required material precondition. Formal: above policy and invariants specified; not model-checked. Empirical: inherited A3/A5/B2 observations only; **no fresh C05 tests executed**. Highly predictable: selective finer checks may improve narrow geometry fidelity with less work than always fine; remains unmeasured. Uncertain: tuning thresholds, false rejections, refinement consistency under contact motion, worst-case runtime; unresolved: end-to-end B3/B4/B5 physical realization and integrated A6 tests.
Mandatory gates: independent oracle for narrow clearance/support/moving barrier; blocked/pending on unknown; revision races; deterministic refinement identity; paired complexity and actual runtime; full engine GCC/Clang/sanitizer CI and reviewer sign-off.
