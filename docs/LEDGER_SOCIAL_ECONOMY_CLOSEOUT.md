## STEWARD — reconciled foundations

Keep the following as governing distinctions:

- structure is not a separate substance above interaction; concrete situated relations are real instantiations of structure;
- a concrete micro relation may instantiate a macro structured totality without exhausting it;
- identity of constitution does not imply identity of scale, form, function or determination;
- macro and micro are constitutively connected but analytically nonidentical;
- existence precedes essence: relations, traces, consequences and memories may objectively exist before any actor or diagnostic successfully names their higher-order meaning;
- memory may be objectively instantiated rhizomatically across actors, objects, signs, routes, infrastructure, routines, ecological traces and records;
- one actor may be their own sufficient expectation through self-stigmergic recurrence;
- `server truth != individual knowledge != group report` remains binding;
- no authoritative ecological-balance, institutional, legitimacy, class-power or structural-violence scalar is introduced.

Logical maxim:

> **Mutual nonidentity does not prevent compound instantiation.**

Scale maxim:

> **Identity of constitution does not imply identity of form, scale, function, or determination.**

## Gradient + memory social extension

The accepted stochastic Lifeworld vocabulary now extends cleanly into social and economic reproduction:

- **Uniform = WHEN** — regular opportunity/time substrate.
- **Random = MAYBE** — seeded contingency among plausible alternatives.
- **Gradient = WHAT IS HERE / WHAT IS EASIER OR HARDER NOW** — present resource, access, risk, social, institutional and informational relations bias practical possibilities.
- **Memory = WHAT WAS HERE / WHAT PRIOR ACTION MADE EASIER OR HARDER** — past actions leave material, social, ecological, economic or semiotic consequences that alter future gradients.
- **Cluster = BECAUSE** — repeated local concentration or social pattern is ordinarily the consequence of gradients + memory + feedback, not a third arbitrary sampler.

Social recurrence:

`opportunity -> situated action -> stigmergic/material consequence -> memory -> changed gradient -> future action`

Collective recurrence:

`action_A -> changed affordances for B -> action_B -> changed affordances for C -> ... -> reproduced collective pattern`

Structural reproduction:

`relation -> consequence -> memory -> gradient -> recurrence -> structured totality`

Gradient conditions possibility; it does not dictate action:

`P(action | gradient) < 1`

## Kinetic stigmergy

Kinetic stigmergy is accepted as a first-class feature rather than merely an AI possibility.

Core relation:

`movement -> affordance capture -> world alteration -> trace -> momentum-preserving release`

Player-facing maxim:

> **Move through the world; write on the world.**

Implementation direction:

- reuse Fuzzy Rail capture/release and Relational Physics;
- keep a small intentional verb vocabulary with broad contextual affordances;
- compatible world-changing actions remain snappy and momentum-preserving in the Jet Set Radio sense;
- physically justified traces may be created, erased, redirected, concealed, repaired or overwritten through ordinary action;
- stylize evidence for rapid readability without converting uncertainty into hidden authoritative answers;
- allow signs/marks as semiotic stigmergy whose meaning is actor-, history- and context-relative;
- actors may learn compact semantic trace/consequence associations;
- learned information may spread through reports, demonstration and shared artifacts, but cognition is never copied directly.

## Objective memory / situated interpretation

The world may preserve objective causal residues without one canonical social interpretation.

`objective past != one authoritative meaning of the past`

Two actors may form incompatible but materially grounded reconstructions of the same historical continuum.

A memory may remain real and efficacious through distributed manifestations even where no actor reconstructs exactly the same essence from them.

Do not require a universal semantic event ID for every downstream trace. Technical IDs remain legitimate for networking/debugging/persistence where machinery requires them.

## Convention, culture and institution

Accepted continuum:

`memory -> habit -> self-stigmergy -> convention -> institution`

No categorical magic transition is required.

### Convention

A repeated sign/practice becomes socially conventional when socially connected actors independently demonstrate compatible interpretation through successful action.

A convention is a defeasible prior, not a command.

Different groups may assign different meanings to the same physical sign. Meanings may be misunderstood, translated, appropriated, hybridized or deliberately manipulated.

### Institution

Recursive social expectation is sufficient but not ontologically necessary for all durable practice; a single actor may sustain a self-referential institution-like practice through memory and recurrence.

A convention becomes institutionally stronger when actors act partly on expectations about other actors' expectations and when practices become redundantly externalized into artifacts, topology, routines, infrastructure, signs and transmitted use.

Institutional memory is distributed across world and actors rather than stored in a faction mind.

### Passive and active drift

**Passive drift:** institutions/practices change because environment, access, population, infrastructure, rhythms, ecology or material supports change without an actor deliberately redesigning the institution.

**Active drift:** actors deliberately repair, reinterpret, appropriate, contest, sabotage, restore, redirect or exploit inherited practices.

Both can occur simultaneously.

Transformation is proven by changed reproduction, not merely intention or symbolism:

`intervention -> changed relations -> changed reproduction -> changed future possibilities`

## Structure, situated activity and structural violence

Do not model structure as a hidden object that situated activity merely causes.

A situated activity can itself be a concrete instantiation of a structural relation while remaining nonidentical to the exhaustive macro totality.

Thus:

`Actor != Structure`

while a concrete relation may satisfy:

`SituatedActivity(actor_i, actor_j, context) = one concrete existence/determination of StructuralRelation`

The actor remains a whole person with other possible relations, intentions and determinations.

### Structural violence criterion

An institutional/social/economic relation becomes a candidate case of structural violence when its ordinary reproduction persistently organizes asymmetric sustainable possibilities through relations such as:

- constrained access;
- reproduced dependency;
- transferred cost or risk;
- suppressed/weak practical alternatives;
- reduced contestability/transformative capacity;
- durable advantage for another actor/group/position;
- recurrence through ordinary social/material reproduction rather than one isolated hostile act.

**Severity is not identical to structural character.** Harsh-but-ordinary normalized arrangements can instantiate structural violence, and sometimes relatively soft recurring relations can also satisfy the structural criterion. Conversely, a severe isolated event need not constitute a reproduced structural relation.

Do not store `structural_violence = X` as world truth.

### Personification

An actor does not merely represent or carry structural violence from elsewhere. Their situated activity may itself be the structural relation in one concrete moment.

Defeating, persuading, bypassing or cooperating with a personified actor changes structural violence only insofar as subsequent reproductive relations change. Replacing one actor with another while access, dependency, incentives and alternatives remain identical is displacement, not structural transformation.

## Collective action without hive mind

Collective action begins when actors become part of one another's conditions of action through changed affordances, commitments, traces, reports, expectations or material support.

Many similar independent actions are not automatically collective action.

Accepted distinct forms include:

1. stigmergic coordination;
2. reciprocal coordination;
3. conventional coordination;
4. explicit shared-intention/commitment coordination.

All may be genuinely collective in different senses; they are not identical.

Collective action becomes structurally reproductive when it deposits enough causal memory to create gradients that make future collective capacity easier to reproduce, potentially after the initiating actors are gone.

## Human Crews

Human factions are **Crews**, not fixed socioeconomic classes.

Crews resemble overlapping forms of:

- pirate communities;
- maroon communities;
- unions/guilds;
- clubs;
- gangs;
- sports teams;
- cultural scenes.

Members may be part-time culture workers, part-time mercenaries and participants in other ordinary jobs/roles.

Crew affiliation and class position are nonidentical.

A Crew can be advantaged in one relation and disadvantaged in another. Individual members may simultaneously participate in equal, Rawlsian unequal and non-Rawlsian unequal relations.

Faction membership, commitment, expectation and situated action remain distinct; dissent does not automatically erase Crew membership.

## Rawlsian / non-Rawlsian inequality and class struggle

Working game-theory abstraction accepted for this project:

- equal outcomes/relations do not constitute class struggle on that dimension;
- Rawlsian inequality does not constitute class struggle on that dimension;
- non-Rawlsian inequality constitutes a **micro-economic class struggle** for the situated person affected;
- when materially similar non-Rawlsian relations recur across actors occupying a reproducible structural position, the relation becomes a **macro-economic class struggle** for the class/position.

### Rawlsian test

An unequal arrangement is Rawlsian only insofar as it improves the sustainable possibilities of the least-advantaged position relative to materially feasible alternatives **without manufacturing the disadvantage by suppressing alternatives, access or contestability**.

Do not reduce the test to equal outcomes or to mere consent.

### Micro / macro

A local unequal encounter may be a concrete determination of a macro class relation without exhausting that macro totality.

`micro relation != macro totality`

while:

`macro totality is constituted through concrete micro relations and their historical reproduction`

## Bargaining / disagreement points

A bargain may be voluntary and individually rational while still participating in a non-Rawlsian class relation.

Actor `i` estimates disagreement from the best sufficiently viable presently known noncooperative continuation:

`d_hat_i = situated viability estimate(best known viable noncooperative continuation)`

A negotiated outcome may satisfy:

`u_hat_i(x) > d_hat_i`

while the low disagreement point itself has been structurally reproduced through dependency, exclusion, suppressed alternatives or transferred risk.

Consent therefore does not settle the structural question.

## Surplus value as energy-accounting surplus

Accepted correction:

> **Surplus is surplus value of energy accounting.**

Conceptually:

`surplus_value = productive output - costs required to reproduce the activity/participants/maintenance/repair`

The politically significant question is not merely whether surplus exists but how claims and disposition arise.

Relevant relations include:

- contribution;
- maintenance;
- organization;
- skill;
- risk;
- access/control;
- reproduction requirements;
- bargaining alternatives;
- distribution;
- reinvestment.

Disposition of surplus changes future reserves, property, infrastructure, access, disagreement points and gradients.

`production -> reproduction -> surplus value -> distribution/reinvestment -> changed future gradients`

Do not create one universal opaque `surplus_value` master meter if the underlying energy-account flows already preserve the causal relations needed for the decision.

## Wage relations

Unequal compensation is not sufficient for exploitation.

A wage relation becomes exploitative when a contracting/employer position captures surplus value importantly through a reproduced non-Rawlsian bargaining asymmetry rather than through a Rawlsian contribution of organization, capital, infrastructure, scarce skill, stewardship or genuinely borne risk.

An exploited actor may still rationally prefer the offered wage to their current disagreement alternative.

Keep distinct:

`productive contribution != bargaining power != surplus claim != class relation`

## Property

Possession is immediate physical control.

Property is reproducible social/material control supported by expectations, memory, access practices, maintenance, enforcement and recognized claims such that control persists beyond immediate occupation.

Property is relational/institutional; an object need not possess an ontological `PROPERTY` essence independent of these relations.

## Commons

Refinement locked:

A commons is either:

1. **spontaneous/self-existing commons** — a natural/material domain whose existence and ordinary regenerative persistence substantially reproduce shared availability; or
2. **methodically stewarded commons** — a commons whose continued availability is maintained by an interested collective such as a union, guild, Crew, cooperative or local association responsible for that segment of the commons.

A commons is not primarily "free loot for everyone" and not simply "unowned property."

### Stewardship and governance

Maintenance becomes governance when continued stewardship requires recurring decisions over access, allocation, repair, restraint, conflict or investment that condition other users' future possibilities.

A guild/union may govern a segment of a commons without owning the entire commons absolutely.

### Enclosure

Restriction is not automatically enclosure. Restrictions may preserve common sustainability.

Enclosure develops when control of stewardship/access begins reproducing exclusion or dependency primarily for the steward/controller's positional advantage rather than the sustainable reproduction of the commons and legitimate users.

Enclosure may arise gradually through passive institutional drift as well as deliberate capture.

## Accumulation and class power

Unequal reserves do not automatically constitute class power.

Accumulation becomes class power when stored surplus recursively reshapes other actors' disagreement points, access, dependencies, risk exposure or practical alternatives.

Maxim:

> **Wealth is stored capacity. Class power begins when stored capacity reshapes other people's possibility-space.**

## Rent

Not every payment for access is exploitative rent.

A stewardship/access payment can be Rawlsian where it finances maintenance/investment that improves the least-advantaged viable position relative to feasible alternatives.

Rent becomes structurally extractive when control of a scarce/necessary access relation enables claims on surplus value beyond the legitimate reproduction of stewardship, maintenance, productive contribution and genuinely borne risk, especially where scarcity/dependency is manufactured or preserved to protect the claim.

Useful diagnostic question:

> **Does the claimant reproduce the valuable affordance, or reproduce the scarcity surrounding the affordance?**

The answer can be mixed.

## Credit and debt

Credit is potentially useful intertemporal cooperation: present energy/surplus/resources are transferred in exchange for a legitimate future claim.

Credit becomes class power when accumulated claims on future surplus recursively change borrowers' disagreement points, access, risk allocation or available activity.

Debt becomes exploitative when repayment claims suppress feasible alternatives beyond what is justified by the lender's actual sacrifice, risk and contribution, producing non-Rawlsian dependency.

Canonical problematic loop:

`debt -> future claim -> reduced alternatives -> worse disagreement point -> more dependency/debt`

No debt essence or morality meter is required.

## Risk

Risk allocation should follow the actual relation among:

- control;
- expected benefit;
- contribution;
- legitimate knowledge;
- exposure;
- capacity to absorb loss.

Do not automatically dump failed-venture risk onto labor, borrower, employer or investor by role label alone.

## Insurance / mutual aid

Pooled insurance or mutual aid becomes commons-like when pooled reserves/surplus are methodically stewarded to reproduce members' sustainable resilience against distributed risk rather than principally generating leverage over members who later require assistance.

This is a legitimate stewarded-commons pattern when its governance and disposition preserve shared resilient possibility.

## Mercenary contradiction

A Crew's paid work may materially reproduce a class relation its members culturally or politically oppose.

The contradiction exists through the concrete relation even before actors recognize it.

Possible responses after recognition include refusal, renegotiation, compensation, alternative provisioning, sabotage, organizing, exit or continued participation under weak disagreement alternatives.

No morality tree is required.

## SIGNAL — paused handoff / next three questions

The current round stops here. Resume with these three questions rather than reopening accepted contracts:

### Q1 — What is money relative to universal energy accounting?

**Recommended direction:** money should be a transferable/socially recognized accounting claim over access to present/future resources, services and surplus, not literal physical energy and not a second incompatible universal currency. Determine when direct energy-accounting truth and socially recognized monetary claims diverge.

### Q2 — When are dues, taxes or levies legitimate stewardship rather than non-Rawlsian extraction?

**Recommended direction:** legitimacy should depend on how contributions are methodically stewarded into commons/public goods, burden distribution, least-advantaged sustainable possibility, transparency/contestability and viable alternatives—not on the label `tax`, `dues` or `fee`.

### Q3 — When should bankruptcy, restructuring or debt forgiveness supersede strict repayment?

**Recommended direction:** when continued enforcement would reproduce a non-Rawlsian collapse of viable alternatives and restructuring/write-off produces a more sustainable reproductive relation than collection, while preserving appropriate consequences for strategic deception or transferred risk through ordinary evidence rather than a morality flag.
