## Literary / philosophical / scientific synthesis

- **Focalization:** every actor possesses a bounded epistemic horizon; world truth is not narrator knowledge.
- **Polyphony:** multiple incompatible but locally rational accounts can coexist.
- **Diegesis:** world cues carry ordinary meaning; diagnostics explain rather than author truth.
- **Count-as-one:** Pass I provisionally organizes heterogeneous design traces without erasing their provenance.
- **Metastability:** integration resolves enough tension for action without closing future possibility.
- **Incomplete-information game theory:** actors best-respond to their own beliefs rather than global state.
- **Ecological contest / foraging abstraction:** interaction value depends on resource/relational value, expected costs, opportunity cost, and future options.
- **Metabolic distinction:** health is relational functional viability; energy is metabolic/resource accounting that helps reproduce function.

## Major resolved vertical rules

- Player owns perceptual aim intent; server owns shot truth.
- Viewmodel/world weapon share one combat state.
- Immediate hip-fire remains primary; focus/ADS is never required for competent fire.
- Hitscan small-arms resolution feeds impact, contamination, sound, and evidence.
- Hit and miss both have legitimate informational consequences.
- Evidence channels remain epistemically distinct.
- Reports are evidence, not commands.
- Relays cannot gain unearned precision or certainty.
- Contradictory evidence is represented as bounded competing hypotheses.
- Shared machinery does not imply shared information.
- Cattlers remain on an ecological perception/signaling path rather than human communications.
- Individual tactical AI is derived through nested actor/relation/Crew/locality accounts.
- Target NONE and non-combat interactions remain legitimate.
- Tactical AI emits intent; it does not teleport or write positions directly.
- Human proof requires physical consequence, not debug labels alone.
- At least two Rivals must demonstrate different legitimate knowledge and different embodied actions in the same encounter.

## Positive criticism / collaboration guardrail

Constructive positive criticism is a standing team value. Collaboration, compromise, competition, accommodation, and avoidance/deferral are treated as context-dependent strategic modes. Praise protects successful inheritance; criticism identifies risks and tests without becoming punitive correction.

## Primary risks identified by Pass I

### Hidden regression

Accidentally restoring a global/shared target state while wiring communications, or bypassing movement/physics by assigning AI positions directly.

### Balance

Nested route/Crew/resource value may initially bias AI too strongly toward guarding, as the Pac-Man stress test suggested. Treat this as parameter evidence, not an architectural failure.

### Presentation

Sophisticated causality may remain invisible enough that legitimate inference looks like cheating or randomness.

### Scope

Avoid broad weapon expansion, generalized GOAP, full physical acoustics, generalized trust/diplomacy, universal ballistics, or production art before the vertical causal chain is proven.
