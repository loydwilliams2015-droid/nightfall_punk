## Side principle — Health as relational viability; energy as metabolic accounting

### Canonical distinction

**Health is not a stored substance. Health is an emergent relation of functioning.**

`Health_i = F(systemic, locomotor, manipulator, sensory, structural/material, contamination, environmental relations)`

Damage therefore means degradation of relations the body can maintain with the world. A scalar health projection may exist for compatibility/debugging, but it is not the authoritative ontology.

**Energy is metabolic accounting.** More precisely, metabolic resources/metabolites and environmental inputs feed transformations that support work, regulation, repair, movement, combat, and continued organization.

`MetabolicResources -> EnergyTransformation -> Work / Regulation / Repair -> FunctionalCapacity -> RelationalHealth`

A bounded game account may use:

`DeltaE = Intake + Production + TransferIn - BasalCost - MovementCost - CombatCost - RepairCost - RegulationCost - TransferOut`

Do not conflate metabolites/resources with energy itself. Resources such as glucose-like fuel, oxygen-like oxidants, nutrient/material inputs, stored reserves, and waste/contamination occupy different roles in a metabolic network even when the game abstracts them into a small number of accounts.

### Recursive consequence

Poor relational health can reduce metabolic/resource acquisition and transformation:

`Contamination -> LocomotorLoss -> LowerResourceAccess -> LowerEnergyIncome -> ReducedRepair -> FurtherFunctionalLoss`

Conversely, strong metabolic position can enable repair/recovery without rewinding material history.

### AI implication

An actor need not ask only `how much health do I have?` It may evaluate whether present metabolic/resource flows can reproduce current and anticipated commitments across nested actor/relation/Crew/locality accounts.

This permits strategically distinct states such as:

- high present functional capacity but metabolically unsustainable;
- damaged functional capacity but metabolically well-positioned for recovery;
- locally costly repair that preserves long-horizon Crew/locality viability;
- withdrawal that improves future metabolic/resource access and therefore reproductive victory.

### Compact maxim

**Health = relational viability.**
**Energy = metabolic accounting.**
**Damage = degradation of viable relations.**
**Repair = metabolically costly reconstruction of those relations.**

No compile is implied by this design lock.
