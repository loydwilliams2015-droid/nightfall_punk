## v1.1 inheritance

The v1.1 contamination / relational-physics work inherits the project's 15-minute Sustainable Crescendo rule:

`PLAY / CALM -> BUILD -> PEAK -> RESOLVE -> REOPEN`

The ~15-minute unit is elastic rather than clockwork. Spike, rush and crescendo events remain permitted, including severe Cattler pressure, competitive convergence, contamination crises and other high-amplitude events.

A stronger peak incurs greater **intensity debt** and therefore a greater requirement for aftermath, consequence, recovery, reorientation or low-pressure systemic agency before another major peak is preferred.

## Relational-physics consequence

Pacing should emerge from the same material world rather than from arbitrary encounter scripting. Contamination, stasis, recovery, environmental traces, resource pressure, Rival/Cattler interaction and local persistence can each contribute to build, peak, resolution and reopening phases.

The pacing governor may change event eligibility, opportunity and pressure, but should not falsify actor knowledge or force every AI actor into synchronized spectacle.

## v1.1 status boundary

- **DESIGN COMMITMENT:** sustainable crescendo pacing and the elastic 15-minute organizational unit.
- **IMPLEMENTED:** existing v1.1 material systems that may later feed the governor.
- **NOT YET CLAIMED:** a complete runtime pacing state machine, intensity-debt implementation, session-intent inference or full adaptive director.

The next implementation step should remain bounded: expose a small pacing-state structure and test one complete `BUILD -> PEAK -> RESOLVE -> REOPEN` cycle without broadening v1.1's contamination proof unnecessarily.
