# nightfall!punk v1.7A — Spatial Logic Laboratory Ledger

## Thesis
A level is a materially simulated field of spatial possibilities. Geometry changes what actors can perceive, reach, risk, contest, escape, remember, and convert into action. Navigation describes physical possibility; actor-local appraisal determines action.

## Universal ownership-contract exchange principle
When an object, resource, capability, responsibility, claim, or authoritative relation changes who or what may control, use, steward, exclude, modify, or account for it, represent that transition where practical as a typed ownership-contract exchange event. The authoritative relation changes through the event; the Universal Cell Grid may retain a weighted contextual memory gradient. Gradient memory never creates authority and a new authority never erases historical context.

Contract means a typed engine relation, not necessarily consent. Transfer, delegation, shared use, abandonment, seizure, capture, revocation, stewardship, and contested claim are all valid relation families.

## Universal Cell Grid
The universal cell is a common relational medium, not a god-object. Material truth, topology, ecology, claims, actor belief, and encounter pressure retain separate authoritative owners. The laboratory implementation uses a fixed 12x12 sparse-friendly cell representation with system-like channels for material solidity, exposure, resource/risk, traversal affordance, support, dirty topology, and contract-memory trace.

## Policy controls
The headless AI-player laboratory compares three policies on identical procedural seeds:
- informed: bounded local observation plus decomposed goal, exposure, ecological risk, resource, option retention, affordance, contract-context and revisit terms;
- greedy: goal-directed shortest-direction control without the richer relational terms;
- random: uniform reachable-action negative control.

No controller reads a universal tactical 'best cell' score. The informed controller is deliberately local and bounded.

## Point / counterpoint interim findings
1. Point: the spatial substrate is deterministic and materially coherent across both population and selected-seed tests. Counterpoint: the current 12x12 laboratories prove a grammar and diagnostic method, not world-scale performance or finished level design.
2. Point: informed bounded appraisal strongly dominates random play and modestly improves population exposure/health over greedy control. Counterpoint: on adversarial selected seeds, greedy can be shorter and slightly cheaper because informed appraisal is still locally myopic and does not yet convert option value into multi-step advantage.
3. Point: perceptual mode preserves 100% completion on the selected set with only a modest aggregate gap from instrumented play. Counterpoint: 14/40 route divergences, including one LOGIC_ONLY case, show that simulated spatial value can outrun player-legible communication.
4. Point: MATERIAL_GAP = 0 supports the ownership rule that topology/affordance cannot override material truth. Counterpoint: high-speed native Snap traversal and dynamic physical interaction still require human and later integrated proof.
5. Point: corridor, cover, vertical, and Snap laboratories show strong informed-vs-greedy discrimination. Counterpoint: loop and dynamic labs expose the absence of multi-step route commitment and topology-risk anticipation.
6. Point: ecology produces the strongest exposure reduction in the population test. Counterpoint: lower exposure accompanied lower remaining health in the ecology lab, so immediate safety, resource profitability, energy sustainability, and long-horizon persistence must remain separate terms rather than one ecology score.
7. Point: ownership-contract exchange and gradient memory preserve authority/history separation. Counterpoint: contract memory currently has weak generic utility; 1.7B needs role-relative meaning for steward, guest, rival claimant, thief, maintainer, forager, etc.
8. Point: random remains a useful negative control. Counterpoint: random is too weak to validate advanced intelligence by itself; greedy and future bounded multi-step controls are required.
9. Point: OITOA passes and produces inspectable failures rather than hiding them. Counterpoint: OITOA is a diagnostic gate, not a substitute for human readability, feel, ecological plausibility, or PAR.
