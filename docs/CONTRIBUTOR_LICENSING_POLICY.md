# nightfall!punk — Contributor licensing policy

**Decision date:** 2026-10-10  
**Status:** policy direction locked; binding CLA text still requires legal review.

## Decision

For future **external code contributions**, nightfall!punk/CommonStav will use a **narrow contributor license agreement (CLA) model rather than relying on DCO alone** if preserving future proprietary/commercial relicensing remains a project objective.

A DCO may still be used as a provenance/sign-off mechanism, but it is not the selected substitute for the additional license grant needed for dual licensing.

## Required properties of the eventual CLA

The intended agreement should:

1. let contributors **retain copyright ownership**;
2. grant the project a non-exclusive, perpetual right to use and redistribute the contribution under GPL-3.0-or-later;
3. grant only the additional sublicensing/relicensing rights actually needed to offer separately negotiated commercial licenses;
4. ensure accepted contributions remain available in the public GPL commons even if a commercial license is also offered;
5. include a contributor representation that they have authority to contribute the work;
6. address contributor-controlled patent rights proportionately;
7. avoid blanket copyright assignment;
8. distinguish individual and employer-owned contributions where necessary;
9. preserve attribution and repository provenance;
10. receive qualified legal review before the first external code contribution is accepted under it.

## Current contribution gate

Until the reviewed CLA text exists, maintainers may accept issues, design discussion, bug reports, test reports, and review, but should **not merge external code contributions** that would complicate future relicensing rights.

## Why not DCO alone?

The DCO is a certification of provenance/authority to submit. It does not by itself grant the project the broader relicensing rights contemplated by a GPL + commercial dual-license model.

This policy is not itself the contributor agreement and should not be presented as one.
