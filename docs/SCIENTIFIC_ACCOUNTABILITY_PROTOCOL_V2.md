# nightfall!punk — Scientific Accountability Protocol v2

**Effective:** 2026-10-10  
**Scope:** project-wide research, code, demos, PAR comparisons, AI-player studies, releases, and human tests.  
**Relationship:** extends, does not erase, the 2026-10-04 1.8A protocol.

## Evidence classes

- **DESIGN / H0:** coherent, falsifiable specification; no executable proof implied.
- **H1 LAB:** compiled deterministic/synthetic fixtures with declared platform and limits.
- **H2 COMPARATIVE:** matched controls, ablations, held-out cases, denominators, and uncertainty reported.
- **H3 RUNTIME:** committed source, branch-specific CI, native authoritative runtime integration, and measured performance.
- **H4 PLAY:** consented player-facing trials with declared instrumentation and qualitative/quantitative analysis.

A `LOCK` records a decision at the stated level. It does not promote itself.

## Mandatory record

Every milestone claiming evidence must record: exact source identity (commit or archive SHA-256), ancestry, toolchain/platform, configuration, independent seed count versus repeated rows, fixtures/oracles/tolerances, pass/fail outcomes, failures and exclusions, raw-data identity, known unsupported scope, and the reviewer/PR or other acceptance record.

## Scientific integrity rules

1. **Separate logical necessity from empirical support.** A priori means conditional on adopted axioms.
2. **Preserve negative evidence.** Failed seeds and repaired bugs remain referenced; corrections are append-only errata.
3. **No metric substitution.** Work units are not milliseconds; estimated impulses are not applied momentum; benchmark score is not player feel.
4. **No population inflation.** Repeated rows from one authored world are not independent worlds.
5. **Keep challengers.** Incumbents require severe counterpoints and held-out disagreements.
6. **Observer independence.** Camera/debug/UI/analysis state never becomes world authority.
7. **Privacy by design.** Do not commit credentials, private server logs, identifying telemetry, or unconsented recordings.
8. **Reproducibility before rhetoric.** Preserve scripts, source hashes, raw rows where practical, and exact build flags.
9. **Claim language follows evidence.** Say "H1 laboratory candidate," not "production proven," when that is what the test supports.
10. **AI-assisted work is reviewable work.** Record substantive AI assistance; human acceptance remains a distinct action.

## PAR discipline

PAR is comparative adequacy, not an acronym and not a global ranking. Each PAR claim names the peer/reference mechanism, dimension, nightfall implementation, measurement, difference, proof, and evidence grade. Comparative references are evidence, not imported project premises.

## Correction protocol

When an error is found: preserve the failed run; identify cause; commit the correction separately; rerun the relevant control and challenger; update the ledger with old and new source hashes; never rewrite history to imply the failure did not occur.
