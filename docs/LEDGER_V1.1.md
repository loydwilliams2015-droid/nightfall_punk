## Governing engine thesis

> **Everything is material; not every material relation is the same kind of relation.**

nightfall models one authoritative relational material world through distinct computational modules:

- **physics** — force, geometry, matter, transfer, structure;
- **ecology** — persistence, metabolism, occupation, recurrence;
- **semantics** — material relations of situated reference/significance;
- **social ecology** — materially instantiated relations among actors, groups, practices, evidence and history.

Semantic modularity is an engineering distinction, not an ontological dualism. Meaning is not painted onto matter; meaning is one organization of material relations.

Reference synthesis:

- **Prototype** — occupation can materially transform territory and acquire a readable phenotype;
- **Oxygen Not Included** — stocks, flows, machines, transfer and dependency networks provide causal substrate;
- **Rain World** — situated organisms inhabit local habitat/topography as participants rather than encounter scripts.

Game translation:

> **A Rain World-like situated ecology operating through Oxygen Not Included-like stocks/flows/machinery and Prototype-like territorial materialization, compressed into an eco-horror Tomb Raider / SWAT / systemic arena FPS.**

Constraint:

> **Do not simulate a property merely because it can be simulated. Simulate it when its material relation can become an affordance, consequence, trace or cause.**

## Wall-of-nonsense safeguard

> **No chain of inference may continue through an unverified semantic premise.**

Analysis order: `VERIFY LEGEND -> OBSERVE -> CROSS-CHECK LOG -> FACT -> INFERENCE -> QUESTION`.

A cluster is not proved by visual proximity in one run. Conditional recurrence under changed conditions must earn `Cluster = BECAUSE`.

## Biological-mathematics continuity

Locked causal vocabulary from v1.0:

- **Uniform = WHEN** — authoritative fixed-step opportunity;
- **Random = MAYBE** — seeded contingency among plausible outcomes;
- **Field = WHAT IS HERE** — actor-legitimate local material evidence;
- **Memory = WHAT WAS HERE** — bounded material history;
- **Semantics = WHAT THESE MATERIAL RELATIONS MEAN TO THIS SITUATED ACTOR**;
- **Cluster = BECAUSE** — conditional recurrence generated by history/topography/feedback, not a third RNG sampler.

Canonical stack:

`clock -> chance -> local material relation -> situated significance -> action -> consequence -> trace -> changed future relation`

## Q1–Q14 locked build contracts

### Q1 — defeat ontology

**LOCKED:** sufficient damage produces **game-mechanical STASIS**, not a declaration of literal biological death.

Agency states: `ACTIVE -> CRITICAL -> STASIS -> REVIVING/RETURNING -> ACTIVE`.

Stasis suspends normal gameplay agency. Presentation may use thriller/psychological near-death imagery, disorientation and contamination without realistic death or gore.

### Q2 — contamination is damage

**LOCKED:** **the contamination system is the damage system.**

Damage is not a conventional HP subtraction with contamination painted on afterward. `health` may remain temporarily as a compatibility/debug projection while authoritative bodily damage migrates into contamination state.

Universal contamination body-horror vocabulary may include dark ooze, staining, smoke/particulate, synthetic residue, filament and signal-like distortion. Restrained PG-13 splatterpunk may occasionally punctuate the image; blood is not the privileged damage indicator.

### Q3 — locality remembers

**LOCKED:** stasis emits a bounded authoritative contamination/defeat trace that persists and decays after revival.

> **The character recovers; the locality remembers.**

### Q4 — revival

**LOCKED:** revival restores agency but does not rewind material/social/ecological history and does not restore prior inventory possession automatically.

### Q5 — inventory fate

**LOCKED:** stasis breaks active inventory possession through three fates:

1. **BASE RESTOCK** — an equivalent standardized item may later be issued; this is not persistence of the original object;
2. **SITE PERSIST** — materially interesting objects remain associated with the stasis locality and provenance;
3. **ECOLOGICAL ABSORPTION** — a discrete item may cease to be individually simulated while materially relevant consequence is conserved as local accounting/trace.

`despawn` should normally mean a change of representation, not erasure of causality.

First greybox fate mapping may be provisional and diagnostic; final item taxonomy is not locked by v1.1.

### Q6 — functional localization

**LOCKED:** contamination uses four broad functional body domains, not anatomical simulation:

- `SYSTEMIC`
- `LOCOMOTOR`
- `MANIPULATOR`
- `SENSORY`

### Q7 — recovery

**LOCKED:** distinguish rapid stabilization, slow bounded natural recovery, deliberate decontamination/treatment, and revival. Actor recovery does not automatically clean the environment.

### Q8 — transfer

**LOCKED:** contamination may transfer through bounded material relations: body/surface contact, water, limited airborne transport and appropriate body-to-body contact. Transfer depends on phenotype, concentration, exposure, susceptibility and time; not every puddle is automatically infectious.

### Q9 — affordance consequence

**LOCKED:** contamination becomes mechanically important when it changes a material relation or affordance.

Examples: locomotor contamination modifies movement; manipulator contamination modifies handling; sensory contamination modifies perceptual reliability; surface contamination changes evidence/maintenance/traction where implemented; machine contamination may alter throughput where implemented.

> **Damage is degradation of relationships the body can maintain with the world.**

### Q10 — equipment / armor

**LOCKED:** equipment mediates contamination/exposure; it is not a second HP bar. Equipment can itself become contaminated or damaged.

### Q11 — accounting conservation

**LOCKED:** transfer uses bounded accounting conservation. Moving contamination debits a source and credits a receiver; removal/decay/transformation is an explicit sink. Literal molecular conservation is out of scope.

### Q12 — environmental cleaning

**LOCKED:** ordinary environmental processes can redistribute/remove contamination: water/drainage, ventilation, maintenance and bounded decay. Avoid a monotonically dirtier world.

### Q13 — contamination vs infestation

**LOCKED:**

> **Contamination is universal body horror / the universal damage system. Infestation is Dream Cattler-specific ecological organization.**

Cattlers suffer/produce/transport contamination like other embodied actors but may tolerate, interpret and reorganize it differently. Contamination alone never proves or creates infestation. Cattler occupation/history remains required.

### Q14 — first v1.1 proof

First bounded human/automated proof:

`combat -> localized contamination -> functional consequence -> STASIS -> inventory fate accounting -> persistent trace -> revival without rewind -> bounded environmental transport/decay -> continued world history`

Do not claim a semantic/social behavioral response until the relevant actor evidence path is actually wired and demonstrated.

`WHITE = PLAYER | BLUE = ALLY | RED = RIVAL | ORANGE = CATTLER | YELLOW BANDS = CATTLER WEAK POINT`
