# Registered owner-frame comparison

2026-10-10, new EEC H2 experiment, before corpus execution. Baseline f30ef6b;
handoff f377d662. M4 incumbent, M3 mandatory paired challenger.

Thirty-two canonical, 256 randomized, 64 pathological, 64 boundary and 256
held-out indexed fixtures; 672 fixture IDs, two models, three dependent timing
repeats. Calibration PRNG seed 0xa6b218b2; separate held-out seed 0xeec018b2.
Rotate model execution order by case/repeat. No concurrent compilation while
measuring. Retain failed raw data; a held-out failure is a failed gate, never
silently retune against that seed. Boundary IDs deliberately repeat discrete
boundary configurations; do not count them as unique physical worlds.

Independent construction enumerates: eligible without contact, revoked
credential, missing material, changed contract after assessment, withdrawal,
failed publication, full receipt storage, changed object revision, moving
support, absent inventory, closed precondition, and obstructed actor hull.
Boundary fixtures use exact reach and one ULP below the constructed separation.
Pathological fixtures additionally include nonfinite actor height and position.
Random/held-out fixtures vary actor position within known canonical coverage.
Expected grant/debit/slot/publication and state are authored before evaluating
the models. The ten separate EEC fixtures independently cover actual applied
A4 contact, interruption/history retention, torn physical WAL, and retry.

Measure proposal CPU time and one complete real A6 owner tick at dt=1/60,
including revalidation, physical staging, and registry publication; initialization
and CSV output are outside timing. Report p50/p95/p99 and 16.667ms CPU frame
overruns. These are headless owner CPU timings, not display or player latency.
The additional authenticated network experiment measures server receipt-to-tick
disposition in integer milliseconds, and legacy snapshots demonstrate actual
movement. It cannot establish human perception or client encounter UI quality.

Mandatory comparison gates: zero authored mismatches, zero unsafe publication,
zero debit/slot conservation violations, identical semantic replay and paired
M3/M4 outcomes, no invented A4 history in the one-frame no-contact corpus.
Physical causal precision/recall comes from the separate positive-contact EEC
fixtures, not from dividing by zero positives in this corpus. Balanced selection
considers full-owner p95 and overruns after correctness gates, with a preference
for retaining M4 if its whole-tick cost offers no decisive reason to switch.
Live multi-actor execution, B3 realization, B5 registry crash recovery and human
acceptance remain separately graded; this experiment cannot close those gates.
