# Creative direction note — Rollins × Bergensten
Project: **nightfall!punk**. Date: 2026-10-20. Status: **proposed major cultural / engineering direction**, not a production acceptance or authorization to use third-party intellectual property.

## Intent
Use **Henry Rollins** and **Jens Bergensten** as reference points for two complementary disciplines:
- **Rollins spirit** — hardcore DIY cultural energy, independent production and publishing, hard work that creates opportunities for others, direct expression, artistic courage, and resistance to empty hype. Preserve a place for dissent and eccentricity without celebrating abuse, burnout, or performative hostility.
- **Bergensten spirit** — pragmatic iterative game systems, emergent interactions, player exploration, careful stewardship of a living sandbox, collaborative development, and accessibility for a broad creative community. Focus on the work and the system rather than celebrity.
- **Combined direction** — punk creative autonomy **plus** durable, understandable, testable, modifiable player-world systems; a serious commons enterprise that can support legitimate commercial work and contributors' rights.

## Operating implications
1. Small, playable, falsifiable prototypes ahead of theatrical promises; repeated Pac-Man-style and encounter benchmarks.
2. Strong authorial identity with an extensible, contributor-friendly engine: one authoritative causal world and heterogeneous creative expressions.
3. Incremental iteration, accessible contributor pathways, reproducible provenance and explicit release gates.
4. Open-source/copyright compliance and reciprocal commons stewardship. Commercial sustainability is welcome under actual license obligations.
5. Creative ambition without compulsory crunch; sustainability and care matter as much as productive intensity.
6. Encourage meaningful freedom for players, modders, contributors and cooperating projects, with intentionally designed constraints rather than arbitrary restrictions.
