## Q722 — KEEP + AMEND: Eco-Tech Guard carbine language

Follow the prior synthesis, but replace the "repair-tool" framing with **eco-tech guard**.

The weapon should remain immediately legible as a functional carbine, but its visual/conceptual identity should imply a protective ecological/infrastructural role without pretending that its coercive or violent capacity does not exist.

The ambiguity should be **hauntological rather than Orwellian**:

- the object may carry traces of maintenance, ecological stewardship, infrastructure, civil defense, field engineering, and older technological futures;
- those traces partially veil the weapon's combat role, but do not linguistically erase or morally sanitize it;
- the player should sense layered histories and unrealized futures in the object, rather than encounter an institutional euphemism that falsely renames violence as harmless service.

Canonical shorthand:

`Readable Carbine -> Eco-Tech Guard Function -> Haunted Infrastructural Residue -> Combat Capability`

not:

`Weapon -> Euphemistic Repair Tool -> Denial of Violence`

### Visual grammar

Preserve the recommended synthesis:

- recognizable carbine proportions and firing affordances first;
- exposed functional geometry;
- restrained asymmetry;
- ecological/infrastructural modules, housings, seals, service points, field-repair interfaces, or material cues;
- mild retrofuture/obsolete-future residues rather than glossy generic military futurism;
- provisional materials/textures during the proof build.

The design can feel as though it descended from a class of devices intended to guard, stabilize, monitor, repair, or maintain ecological/infrastructural systems and was always also capable of force.

### Semantic guardrail

"Eco-tech guard" is a design-language category, not necessarily the final literal in-world product name.

The object should **hide meaning partially, not falsely**:

`Ambiguity = layered function + historical residue + incomplete disclosure`

not:

`Ambiguity = semantic laundering`

Governing maxim:

> **Haunt the weapon with other possible functions; do not pretend it is not a weapon.**

## Q723 — KEEP: Restrained Confirmed Relation Cue

Follow the prior recommendation.

Use a very small authoritative hit marker plus a restrained transient audio cue on confirmed hits. Impact, material response, contamination/body reaction, and world consequence remain primary.

A slight variant may communicate a confirmed precision/weakpoint success when the authoritative hit-zone result supports it.

Do not use floating damage numbers in ordinary play.

Canonical relation:

`Authoritative Hit -> Diegetic Impact/Reaction + Small Confirmed Relation Cue`

The abstract cue means only that the player's shot produced the expected target relation. It must not reveal hidden target state, exact damage math, unseen identity, or other information not legitimately available to the player.
