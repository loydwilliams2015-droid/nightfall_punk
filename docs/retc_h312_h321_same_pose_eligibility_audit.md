- Current source B2 **BLOCKED** or **PENDING** at contact pose: an observed production feasibility obstacle, NOT a passing positive eligibility result; preserve and explain.
- Current source B2 **ELIGIBLE** for both, with source-valid plan: bounded research proof of current two-grant eligibility, NOT owner-issued consumption or atomic commit.
- A stale old grant remaining eligible after pose edit, or changing A6 accepted frame/tick/B3 receipts in the isolated diagnostic: semantic authority or transactional defect; immediate C2 P* HOLD and source correction.
