# v0.9 — Topographical Energy Accounting

## Thesis

nightfall!punk's ecological engine uses **energy accounting as its universal currency**. Game-theoretical actors may pursue different strategies, but all material gains, losses, opportunity costs, regenerative limits and capacity changes eventually settle against one energetic accounting surface.

The engine is explicitly **topographical**. Energetic opportunity depends on where resources and actors are located in the physical landscape: distance, route depth, terrain resistance, elevation, exposure, infrastructure and historically changing regional condition participate in acquisition cost.

Core maxim:

> **The accountant is omniscient; the players are not.**

The authoritative world may know the present energy state. It may not inject that truth into individual cognition. Actor decisions must be based on legitimate observations, reports, priors, confidence and stale beliefs.

## C18 — universal energy accounting

- energy accounting is the universal economic currency
- the same currency permits different games and strategies
- possession is not capacity
- topography participates in price formation
- history continually reprices the landscape
- sustainable growth grows energetic command while reproducing the conditions that allow future growth

## C19 — Bayesian helicopter cycle

Conceptual cycle:

`TRUTH -> ACCOUNT -> SEARCH -> BELIEF -> STRATEGY -> SIMULTANEOUS ACTION -> CONSEQUENCE -> NEW TRUTH`

World-level helicopter search may inspect authoritative present state. Future trajectories remain probabilistic because autonomous actors choose simultaneously and because history changes conditions.

Actor-level search is not omniscient. It searches over an actor's belief surface. Search depth may value immediate, round, regional or regenerative consequences, but those are horizons over one ledger rather than four currencies.

## C20 — accounting invariants

The prototype recognizes three foundational operations:

1. **Transfer** — energy changes controller/location. Total stored energy is conserved by the transfer itself.
2. **Conversion** — potential becomes usable output with recorded loss.
3. **Capacity change** — productive capacity changes future throughput without minting current stock.

Regeneration is treated as an explicit external input to the modeled regional system. Movement and extraction create expenditures/externalities rather than disappearing from the accounting story.

Invariant:

> **Every current gain needs a causal source; every conversion has a cost; apparent multiplication must be capacity or external input, not free energy.**

## C21 — search resolution

The v0.9 implementation deliberately remains bounded:

- 24 coarse regions from the accepted spatial lab
- world metabolism updates inside the fixed simulation step
- global helicopter ranking refreshes at a low cadence rather than solving every object continuously
- topographical route cost uses distance, graph steps, elevation proxy, terrain resistance and exposure
- region truth tracks potential stock, usable stock, production, regeneration, absorption, health, externality load and history
- actor belief tracks mean, confidence and observation age
- belief observation performs a bounded Bayesian-style prior/evidence update
- stale information loses confidence; it does not become magically synchronized with world truth

## C22 — smallest proof

The graybox proof contrasts two localities:

### South Works

- high initial usable stock
- poor regeneration
- weaker absorptive capacity
- higher terrain resistance
- higher topographical cost from the central lab
- sustained occupation can be immediately profitable while degrading the longer-run position

### Signal Court

- lower initial stock
- high productive capacity
- strong regeneration/absorption
- cheaper central access
- sustained future energetic return can become superior to the initially richer locality

The test spawns three Rivals into South Works and advances the authoritative fixed simulation. South Works depletes while Signal Court regenerates. A belief sampled before depletion therefore differs from a belief sampled after history changes the world.

The point is not to prove a final economy. It proves the architectural proposition:

> **Actors can encounter one universal energetic world whose rational opportunities change because topography and history change.**

## World-metabolism prototype

`NfWorld` owns `NfEnergySystem`.

Each region currently tracks:

- baseline potential
- potential stock
- usable stock
- productive capacity
- regenerative capacity
- absorptive capacity
- health
- externality load
- terrain resistance
- elevation index
- structural exposure
- prior/posterior yield
- uncertainty
- recent/cumulative extraction and regeneration

Each active actor can receive an internal energy account tracking reserves, income, expenditure and productive capacity. In v0.9, regional occupancy performs a deliberately simple graybox extraction flow so history can materially change regional availability without waiting for the final resource-interaction UI.

This passive extraction is **not** the final gathering mechanic. It is test machinery for the metabolism/accounting substrate.

## Process fidelity

Long-term ecological/process simulation should use four explicit fidelity levels:

- **embodied** — exact physical simulation where player interaction requires it
- **aggregated** — regional process is numerically simulated
- **presumed** — background process is assumed under a stable causal contract
- **decorative** — no systemic claim

Rule:

> **Presume what cannot profitably be simulated; never presume away consequences.**

Visible world objects can later act as ecological indices, bottlenecks, indicators and process proxies for larger simulated/presumed systems.

## Relationship to earlier contracts

### C6 — simulation for itself

v0.9 implements part of the world-scale layer: consequences and energetic history can continue without requiring every process to be embodied at 60 Hz.

### C7 — plural situated economy

Revised formulation:

> **One world, one universal energy-accounting currency, different strategies and production functions.**

Resources remain relational because the energy return from an object/place depends on actor situation and topography. They are nevertheless commensurable through energetic accounting.

### C10–C11 — information and trust

Information has energetic value when it reduces search, travel, verification, conflict or allocation costs. Trust can therefore become an energetic efficiency mechanism without becoming a separate currency.

### C12–C14 — compacts, norms and tenure

Social order can reduce energetic friction. Access rights, customary routes, commons and temporary compacts can lower conflict/transport cost. The social layer modifies the energy account but does not replace it.

### C16–C17 — history and metabolism

Resources do not generically respawn. They are produced, renewed, converted, transported, consumed, damaged or transformed through world processes. History is stored in present energetic possibility.
