# VPC-01 — Primordial two-actor intentional physics model
**Programme:** nightfall!punk 1.8C.2 RET-C / RET-HK.
**Status:** candidate engineering reference; *not* human-playtest approval or production gameplay.
**Decision:** VPC-01 is the **primary intentional physical-experience acceptance model**. The older greybox remains an inherited regression and control; it is not an adequate substitute for VPC-01 physical/visual correspondence.

## Research hypothesis and physical referent
**Central hypothesis:** Two independently moving embodied actors and a movable environmental object can be perceived as one causally coherent situation when actual contact, movement, obstruction, and interaction consequences agree with the shared authoritative physical/material/contractual state.

The **minimal scene** contains exactly two identifiable moving actors (A and B), one movable pod, a readable ground plane, and environmental spatial references. The initial aesthetic is a modestly brighter postcyberpunk/biopunk adventure direction, maintaining tension without requiring a uniformly dark or grimy world. Any H60 procedural colors, silhouettes or structures are research stand-ins, **not** final creature art or achieved aesthetic fidelity. Television heads are not mandatory.

### Ordered acceptance frontier (do not equate the tiers)
1. **H60 physical research nucleus (implemented as a candidate):** two A4 embodied bodies and one movable pod; bounded horizontal swept-disc geometry; genuine A4 reciprocal linear impulse; motor-driven independent actor proposals; a single staged successor and simple revision-gated publication in a test fixture. 60 Hz, canonical and held-out/negative tests. Explicit PENDING for initial deep penetration and ambiguous simultaneous collision; no unproved contact resolution.
2. **A6/B2/B3 joint-source integration (NOT implemented in H60):** real A6 sole tick ownership, two actor + object authoritative frame, B2 eligibility witnessed for each appropriate action, A5 exact material history, source-backed B3 receipt and one durable physical-contractual transaction. No independent publications, guessed obligations, or authority substitution.
3. **Visible gameplay proof (NOT implemented in H60):** live player controls and visible interaction tied to tier 2, actor-actor plus actor-object and support/traversal tests; source-exact 2+ tick acceptance, rejected/partial/replay paths, and measurable responsiveness. All network/AI/prediction/camera readers obey the accepted snapshot and lifecycle contracts.
4. **VPC-01 human pilot (HOLD):** identical verified playable SHA and scenario; human qualitative evidence from 1 self-observation and optionally 2–3 independent volunteers; consent and unprimed observations; transparent defects and unknowns. Not statistically powered.

H60 executable and core: `src/demo/vpc01_two_actor.c`, `src/shared/nf_vpc01_two_actor.[ch]`, `src/tests/test_vpc01_two_actor.c`. Graphical target: `nightfall_vpc01_two_actor`; CTest: `vpc01_two_actor_primordial_v18c2`. The H60 viewer visually displays this *bounded research* physics (two actors + one pod) and may reject a tick rather than silently continue. It is **not** plugged into the normal dedicated multiplayer server.

## Hypotheses and mandatory gates
- Two actor identities remain distinct; collision impulse and both velocities are derived from A4, not an animated cue.
- Input permutations must stage the same deterministic candidate; repeated seed, fixed tick and source SHA yield identical results.
- An initially deeply overlapping pair, non-finite controls, unsupported geometry, stale revision, and overlapping contact events fail closed, without world publication.
- Single pod PUSH must transfer detectable motion through physical contact, not scripted positional motion.
- Source evidence: GCC + Clang headless regressions and full graphical build, automated X11 render, repeatable SHA; source-exact test logs distinguish successful execution from assumptions.
- What remains **OPEN**: collision manifolds and stacking, vertical support/traversal, A5 material & A6 owner cutover, B2/B3 multi-actor transactional staging, durable recovery, concurrent publication and all game client/AI/network reader migration, finalized VPC-01 art direction, and human qualitative findings.

No p-value is assigned to passing deterministic tests. No volunteer results are invented. If a genuinely empirical effect-size experiment is later warranted, its sampling model, null hypothesis and required precision must be specified prospectively.

**Release holds:** H60 prototype is an engineering benchmark only. Human download, full 1.8C.2 completion, merge to production and 1.8C.3 promotion remain pending separate gates.

## Neutral qualitative protocol
Before any categorical, rating or leading question, give testers the same brief observation prompt from `VPC01_HUMAN_OBSERVATION_PILOT.md`. Facilitation may use only neutral probes, e.g. “Can you say more?” or “What happened next?” They may explore freely; instructions may state the actual controls and material type, not the hypothesis or the intended reaction. Capture uncoached observations first and only afterward conduct a diagnostic bug reproduction interview if needed.
