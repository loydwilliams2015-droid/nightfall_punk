## Q264 — FINALIZED: Sustainable Passage / Reproductive Victory

Replace generic elimination-centered encounter victory with **Sustainable Passage / Reproductive Victory**.

### Core rule

> **Winning means leaving the encounter with a more reproductively viable relation to the world.**

Damage is a means, not an independent victory axis.

### Energy / capacity distinction

`DeltaEnergy = Production + TransferIn - MovementCost - ActionCost - CombatCost - RepairCost - TransferOut`

Damage primarily reduces the capacity to sustain useful relations:

`Capacity_next = Capacity_now - Damage + Repair`

The strategic value of damage is therefore relational:

`DamageValue = OpponentFutureOptionsLost - OwnEnergySpent - ExposureCreated - RepairBurden - EcologicalCost - SocialCost - OpportunityCost`

A tactically successful attack can be strategically losing when it worsens the long-horizon energy account or future-option portfolio.

### Chinese-Checkers contribution

Adopt advancement / positional-transfer grammar rather than capture-as-victory. Other actors and objects can become intermediate affordance infrastructure:

`ActorA -> DeltaWorld -> AffordanceB -> ActionB -> AffordanceC`

This formalizes the standing maxim: **we continuously make possibilities for one another**.

### Underworld contribution

Retain strong adversarial damage pressure so violence remains rational when it changes future viability efficiently. Sustainable accounting must not collapse into pacifism-by-arithmetic.

### Lexicographic encounter evaluation

1. **Objective / transition accomplished**
2. **Resulting viability preserved**
3. **Sustainable energy growth**
4. **Future option value**
5. **Damage / cost efficiency**

Damage cannot compensate for catastrophic objective, viability, or energy failure.

### Legitimate resolution forms

Elimination remains possible, but so do withdrawal, deterrence, route secured, resource acquired or abandoned, exchange completed, extraction, ecological displacement, temporary detente, infrastructure stabilization, and mutual exhaustion.

### Governing loop

`Energy -> Movement/Action -> Position -> AffordanceChains -> Resource/InformationAccess -> Conflict/Exchange -> WorldChange -> NewEnergyFlow`

A strategically progressive solution reproduces or expands its capacity for future action; a degenerating solution may win local firefights while destroying its ability to reproduce those wins.

## v1.5 prebuild implication

The FPS layer should remain immediately legible while the high-leverage junctions become distinctively nightfall!punk: information topography, situated utility, claims, relational resource accounting, world persistence, heterogeneous actor interpretation, and Sustainable Passage / Reproductive Victory.
