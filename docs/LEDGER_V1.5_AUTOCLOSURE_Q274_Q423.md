## v1.5 implementation consequences from Q277–Q418

### Combat / evidence

Server owns shot/damage truth; perceptual aim supplies intent while muzzle/body feasibility remains physical. Hit zones feed contamination domains. Misses, impacts, gunfire and received damage are distinct evidence channels. Suppression acts through exposure/information/counter-affordance pressure, not fake damage. First proof remains carbine + pistol.

### Communications / belief

Reports are fixed-capacity, provenance-bearing, recipient-scoped evidence. Relay cannot improve spatial precision or confidence without new independent evidence. Delivery has bounded latency. Report publication occurs only for meaningful information deltas. Actors retain local competing hypotheses, source-specific memory, confidence decay, uncertainty regions, negative evidence only when observation was legitimately possible, and bounded information-seeking actions.

### Nested tactical AI

Individual tactical utility remains a local expression of nested actor / relation / Crew / locality accounts. Viability constraints precede absurd scalar tradeoffs; no morality meter is introduced. Future-option and success estimates use bounded heuristics rather than full search. Candidate actions are generated from capability + affordance + claims + belief and pruned through relevance/hotspots. `Target = NONE` remains legitimate.

### Sustainable combat ecology

Attack, cover, reposition, guard, withdraw, investigate, assist/revive, route control, resource control and information acquisition compete as interactions. Pursuit decays with cost and uncertainty. Resource value is state-dependent. Cover, routes and information vantage can function as contested resources. Damage remains instrumental rather than a score axis. Encounter resolution can include withdrawal, deterrence, secured/yielded routes, resource transition, extraction, displacement, temporary detente, infrastructure stabilization, or exhaustion.

### Player proof / C architecture

v1.5 requires a visible first-person carbine/pistol state but not final art. One authoritative shot cause drives world and viewmodel presentation. Diagnostics must expose evidence -> belief -> provenance/precision -> nested-account contributions -> selected interaction -> commitment -> embodied consequence. Human proof must show at least two Rivals holding different legitimate knowledge, delayed/coarser relay, a genuine player shot consequence, a low-value non-report case, and at least one rational non-attack action.

No per-tick heap allocation; deterministic seeds; report/provenance stays pure shared C; raylib stays presentation-side; no general GOAP or full Bayesian layer; perception/report/utility updates remain multi-rate and measured.
